Trailing by twelve points heading into the fourth quarter, the visiting team put together a remarkable comeback, scoring on three consecutive possessions.

First came a field goal to get the deficit moving. Then, after forcing a fumble that gave them great field position, the visitors capitalized with a quick touchdown drive. Rather than kicking the extra point, they went for a two-point conversion — and converted it, giving them a one-point lead with just 40 seconds remaining on the clock.

The home team got the ball back for one final chance to respond, but their last drive stalled at midfield, unable to get into scoring range in time. The visiting team held on to win by that single point.
