My grandmother kept a jar full of buttons, and for decades nobody knew the real story behind it. It turns out that whenever a relative passed away, before their clothes were given away or donated, she would quietly cut the buttons off them and save them in the jar. She never told anyone she was doing this — it was a private, secret habit she kept up for years, so the jar slowly filled with little pieces cut from the clothing of people in the family who had died, without any of us realizing what it actually was until much later.
