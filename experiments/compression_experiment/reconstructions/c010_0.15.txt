When people say remote work "destroys company culture," what they usually mean is that it destroys the culture that one particular office happened to build up organically over time — the informal, accidental byproduct of people sharing a physical space every day. That kind of culture was never really designed; it just emerged from proximity. Once you move to remote work, you don't get that for free anymore. Building a strong culture without a shared building is possible, but it doesn't happen automatically — it requires deliberate, intentional effort, and that effort has to be planned for and funded, not just assumed to happen on its own.
