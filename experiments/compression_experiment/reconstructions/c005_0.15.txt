For chocolate chip cookies, use a standard recipe ratio — the usual mix of flour, butter, sugar (both white and brown), eggs, vanilla, baking soda, salt, and chocolate chips that most classic recipes call for. Scoop the dough onto a baking sheet and bake at 375°F for about 9 to 11 minutes. You want to pull them out while the centers still look slightly underdone and soft — they'll continue cooking a bit from residual heat after you take them out of the oven, and this is what gives you that chewy center with crisp edges instead of a dry, overbaked cookie.
