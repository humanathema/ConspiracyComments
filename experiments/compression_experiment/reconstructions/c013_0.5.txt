Train A leaves City A traveling at 60 mph. Train B leaves City B, which is 300 miles away, at the same time, traveling at 40 mph toward City A. The two trains meet after 3 hours: their combined closing speed is 100 mph, and 300 miles divided by 100 mph equals 3 hours — this holds regardless of which train you consider "technically" the one that's moving, since what matters is their speed relative to each other.
