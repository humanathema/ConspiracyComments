Two trains start 300 miles apart and travel toward each other on the same track, one moving at 60 mph and the other at 40 mph. Since they're heading toward each other, their combined closing speed is 60 + 40 = 100 mph. To find out how long it takes for them to meet, divide the distance by the combined speed: 300 miles ÷ 100 mph = 3 hours. So the two trains will meet after 3 hours.
