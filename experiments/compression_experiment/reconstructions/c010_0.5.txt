The common objection that remote work destroys company culture usually means something narrower than it sounds: what it actually destroys is the specific, accidental culture that emerged organically from one particular office's physical layout and the social habits people fell into there. It doesn't mean that no culture at all is possible when working remotely — only that building a culture remotely takes deliberate, unbudgeted effort, rather than happening on its own the way it did in the office.
