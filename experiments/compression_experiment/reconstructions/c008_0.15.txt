For safety, keep the appliance away from water at all times — never operate it near a sink, in the rain, or with wet hands, since combining electricity and water creates a serious shock hazard. Before doing any kind of maintenance or cleaning, always unplug or disconnect it from the power source first. If you notice the power cord is frayed, damaged, or worn, do not attempt to repair it yourself — instead, stop using the device and have it repaired by a qualified professional or replace the cord entirely, since a damaged cord that isn't properly fixed can cause an electric shock or start a fire.
