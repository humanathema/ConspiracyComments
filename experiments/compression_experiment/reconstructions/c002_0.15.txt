Marta arrived at the dock just as the ferry was pulling away, and she'd missed it. She stood there for a moment, expecting to feel panicked or upset, but instead what washed over her was relief — a quiet, guilty kind of relief. The ferry was supposed to take her to a wedding, but if she was honest with herself, she had never really wanted to go in the first place. Missing the boat gave her an excuse she hadn't had to make up herself, and some part of her was grateful for it.
