My grandmother kept a jar of buttons on her windowsill for as long as I can remember. She never explained why it was there, and I never thought to ask — it just seemed like one of those small, ordinary things that old houses accumulate.

It wasn't until after she died that my mother finally told me the truth behind it: every single button in that jar had been cut from a piece of clothing that once belonged to a family member who had passed away. Before donating or giving away their clothes, my grandmother would quietly snip off a button and drop it in the jar — a small, private way of holding on to each person. She had been doing this for forty years, without ever telling anyone.
